The Gutenberg transcription is based on page scans of a 3-volume edition which restarts the chapter numbering with each volume. I have combined the volumes into one and run the chapter numbers sequentially. The original Volume 1 ends at my chapter 18 and Volume 2 ends at my chapter 36. Subtracting these numbers will give you the appropriate chapter in the page scans: eg my chapter 40 matches Volume III, Chapter IV since 40 - 36 = 4.
